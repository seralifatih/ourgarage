Common questions

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. This re-applies any Premium purchase already tied to your Apple ID.

How do I share my garage with my household?

From Settings, tap Share with household and sign in with your Apple ID. You can then invite others with a one-time code that expires after seven days.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email noktadevstudio@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. See our Privacy Policy for details on what happens to shared household data.
