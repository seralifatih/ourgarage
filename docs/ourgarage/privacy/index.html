Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The short version

If you use OurGarage without an account, we collect nothing. Not your email, not your car, not analytics, not a crash report. Your garage lives in a database file on your phone and never reaches us.

You only get an account if you choose to share a garage with your household. That is the only thing that sends any data anywhere, and we only ask for it at the moment you tap “Share with household.”

Who we are

OurGarage is made by Nokta Studio. Contact: privacy@noktastudio.dev

What we collect, and only when

Using the app without an account

Nothing. OurGarage contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, and no advertising SDK, and makes no network request carrying your data. Your vehicles, service history, reminders, odometer readings, costs and notes are stored only in a local database on your device.

You can use OurGarage this way indefinitely. It is not a trial, and no feature of the free, local-only tier stops working after any period of time.

If you create an account to share a garage

An account is requested only when you choose to share a garage with your household — never on first launch, never in the background. Signing in uses Sign in with Apple, the only sign-in method OurGarage offers. At that point we receive and store:

  • Your email address, from Sign in with Apple. If you choose Apple's Hide My Email option, we receive a private relay address and never see your real one.
  • Your display name, if Apple provides it. Apple sends this only on the very first authorization between you and this app, and only if you agree to share it. We use it to name your household by default (for example, “Fatih's garage”). If it is withheld, your household is simply called “My household,” and nothing else about the app changes.
  • A user identifier, generated by our backend provider (Supabase) to represent your account, and shared with our payments provider (RevenueCat) so a purchase can be matched to the household it should unlock.
  • Your garage data — vehicles, service records, reminder rules, odometer readings, costs, and notes — uploaded to our servers so the people in your household can see the same information on their own devices. Data you enter before joining or creating a household is never uploaded unless and until you take that action.

If you buy OurGarage Premium

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit, using our payments provider, RevenueCat, to determine which product you bought and whether your entitlement is currently active. This is how we know a household should have Premium features unlocked. We never receive your card number, billing address, or any other payment details — Apple does not share these with us or with RevenueCat.

What we never do

  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK of any kind, in either the free or the account-based tier.
  • We do not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because we do not do anything that requires it — there is no NSUserTrackingUsageDescription in this app for that reason.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not send marketing email. There is no mailing list.
  • We do not read your location, your contacts, your photos, or your browsing history. OurGarage never asks for any of these permissions.

Who can see your garage

Only the people in your household. Access to shared data is enforced on our servers, per household, using row-level security — not merely hidden by the app's interface — so another OurGarage user cannot see your garage even if they tried to access it directly.

Invite codes expire seven days after they are created and can be redeemed once. As the person who created a household, you can revoke a pending invite or remove a member at any time from within the app.

Removing a member from a household ends that person's access to it. It does not delete the vehicles or service history they could see — that data belongs to the household as a whole, and deleting it because one person left would destroy records the remaining members still rely on.

Where your data is stored

Data from the free, local-only tier is stored exclusively on your device and nowhere else.

If you use household sharing, your garage data, email address, and display name are additionally stored on infrastructure operated by Supabase, our backend provider, and referenced by RevenueCat, our payments provider, for the purposes described above. Both act as processors of your data on our behalf and are contractually restricted from using it for their own purposes.

How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

OurGarage does not currently have an in-app account-deletion or data-export feature. If you want your account and the personal data associated with it deleted, email privacy@noktastudio.dev from the address associated with your account and we will delete it by hand. We reply to deletion requests within 30 days, and typically much sooner. This is a gap we intend to close with an in-app deletion flow in a future release; until then, email is the only way to exercise this right, and we will not make you jump through further hoops to use it.

If you never created an account, there is nothing to delete on our end — uninstalling the app removes everything, since your data was never anywhere but your device.

If you created an account and leave or delete it, shared vehicles and service history belonging to a household you were not the sole member of are not deleted, for the reason described above: they belong to the household, not only to you. If you were the only member of your household, all of its data is deleted along with your account.

Your rights

Whether or not you are covered by a specific privacy law, you can email privacy@noktastudio.dev at any time to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you,
  • get a copy of it,
  • correct anything that is wrong,
  • have your account and personal data deleted (see above).

We do not require a reason, and we reply within 30 days.

If you are located in the UK, EU, or EEA: our lawful basis for processing your data is performance of a contract (providing the household sharing you requested) and legitimate interests (operating and securing the service). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are located in California: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any right it grants you.

Children

OurGarage is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you inside the app before it takes effect. We will never begin collecting a new category of data without updating this policy first.
