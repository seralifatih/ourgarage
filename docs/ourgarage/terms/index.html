4. Household sharing

If you create a household, you are its owner. As owner, you can invite other people to join it, revoke invitations you have not yet sent, and remove members from the household.

Invitation codes expire seven days after they are generated and can be redeemed by one person, one time.

Read this carefully before inviting anyone: every member of a household can see and edit every vehicle, service record, and reminder belonging to that household. There is no per-vehicle or per-record privacy setting within a household. Only invite people you are genuinely willing to give this level of access to your vehicle and maintenance data.

If a member is removed from a household, or chooses to leave one, their access to that household's data ends immediately. Removing a member does not delete the household's vehicles or service history — that data continues to belong to the household as a whole and remains visible to its other members, since deleting it because one person left would take records away from people who still rely on them.
