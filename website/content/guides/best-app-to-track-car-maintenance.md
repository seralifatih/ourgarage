<p class="answer"><strong>Short answer:</strong> there isn't one best app; it depends on what you want to track. If you want fuel economy and every expense, look at Fuelly or Drivvo. If you also want automatic trip tracking, Simply Auto. If you want a free app that pulls in service history reported by shops, CARFAX Car Care. If you want simple reminders by date <em>and</em> mileage with a clean service history, that's what OurGarage is built for (we make it, so weigh that accordingly). For a single date-based reminder, your phone's built-in Reminders app is enough.</p>

This comparison reflects each app's App Store listing as of November 2026. Prices and features change, and some differ by country, so check the current listing before you decide. We've left prices out for that reason.

[[cta]]

## What actually matters in a maintenance app

Before comparing apps, decide which of these you need:

1. **Reminders by date and mileage.** Most intervals are "X miles or Y months, whichever comes first." An app that only does dates will miss the mileage side, and vice versa.
2. **A service history.** Date, odometer reading, work done and cost, so you know what was done and can prove it when you sell.
3. **Fuel and expense tracking.** Useful if you want MPG and total cost of ownership. Unnecessary if you only want to stay on top of services.
4. **Sharing.** If more than one person drives or looks after the car, can they all see and update the same record?
5. **How mileage gets in.** Most apps rely on you typing in the odometer. Some track trips automatically.
6. **Getting your data out.** Can you export or print the history for a buyer?

## The apps, one by one

### CARFAX Car Care

Free. Its main draw is that it can show service history reported to CARFAX by shops, along with service reminders and recall alerts. The listing also mentions an estimated vehicle value.

**Good for:** people who mostly use shops that report to CARFAX.
**Watch for:** work you do yourself, or at shops that don't report, won't show up unless you add it.

### Drivvo

Free for personal use, with a paid Pro tier. It covers a lot: refueling, expenses, preventive and corrective maintenance, income, routes, checklists and reminders. According to its listing, Pro adds cloud backup, sync between devices, no ads and export to spreadsheets. There are also fleet plans for businesses.

**Good for:** people who want to track every cost of running a car, or who use a car for work.
**Watch for:** it does a lot, so there's more to set up if you only want service reminders.

### Fuelly

Free, with an optional premium upgrade. Fuelly started as an MPG tracker: you log miles, gallons and price at each fill-up and it charts your fuel economy, synced with the Fuelly website. It also has service reminders with presets for common jobs such as oil changes and tire rotations.

**Good for:** people who care about fuel economy and fill-up records.
**Watch for:** it's built around fuel-ups, so you get the most from it if you log every fill-up.

### Simply Auto

Free to download with paid upgrades. It combines a fuel log, maintenance reminders by date or mileage, and mileage recording, including automatic GPS or Bluetooth trip tracking as a paid feature. It also supports electric vehicles.

**Good for:** people who want trips recorded automatically as well as maintenance reminders.
**Watch for:** trip and fuel tracking are a big part of the app, which is more than you need if you only want service reminders.

### OurGarage

This is our app, so read this section knowing that. OurGarage does one thing: keep track of what's due on your cars and who did what. You set reminders by date, by mileage or both, log services in seconds (date, odometer, work, cost), and everything works offline with no account unless you choose to share (sharing uses Sign in with Apple). Household sharing, where several people share one garage, is coming in an upcoming update.

Free for one car with unlimited records and reminders. Premium adds more vehicles, cost totals and export (CSV, or a PDF service history per car), with a lifetime or an annual option.

**Good for:** anyone who wants service reminders by date and mileage without tracking fuel.
**Strengths, plainly:** reminders by date, mileage or both, with a service history you can export. Premium is available as a one-time $9.99 lifetime purchase (US price) as well as an annual subscription, so you aren't forced into a subscription. Everything works offline, and without sharing there's no account at all.
**Limits, plainly:** iPhone only. No fuel economy tracking, no automatic mileage (you enter odometer readings, and the app reminds you to when you have mileage-based reminders), no recall alerts, no shop-reported history, and household sharing isn't available yet.

### The free built-in option: Reminders or Calendar

Your phone's Reminders or Calendar app can repeat a reminder every 6 or 12 months. That covers date-based items like registration, and oil changes if you drive little.

**Good for:** one or two date-based reminders.
**Watch for:** it can't do mileage, there's no service history, and sharing a reminder list doesn't give anyone the car's history.

### A paper log or spreadsheet

Still a valid choice. A printed log is free, permanent and easy to hand to a buyer. Our free [car maintenance log template](/guides/car-maintenance-log-template/) has a page for tracking what's due next. Its weakness is that it never reminds you.

## How to choose

- **You want MPG and every expense:** Fuelly or Drivvo.
- **You want trips recorded automatically:** Simply Auto.
- **You want history from shops with no setup:** CARFAX Car Care.
- **Several people share the car, or you look after a family member's car:** OurGarage. See our guide to [sharing car maintenance with family](/guides/share-car-maintenance-with-family/).
- **You only need an oil change reminder:** see [oil change reminder apps](/guides/oil-change-reminder-app/) for what makes one work.

Whichever you pick, the app is only as good as what you put in it. Log each service the day it's done, and keep the odometer reading current.
